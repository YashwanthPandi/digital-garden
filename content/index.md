## 🧭 How to use these notes

- **Start from a notebook index.** It lists topics in a suggested reading order.
- **Follow links between notes.** TypeScript concepts are referenced from Angular notes and vice versa.
- **Use tags** to find related ideas across notebooks, for example `#typescript`, `#angular`, `#oop`, `#rxjs`.
- **Code samples** are meant to be copied and tried out, not just read.
